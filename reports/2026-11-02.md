## TL;DR

- **The big event — a Binance security incident on Oct 29.** The DeFi Investor called it [a hack of the world's largest exchange for $6B+](https://x.com/TheDeFinvestor/status/2116125540893544896) (commentary). Aave [froze every DAO-run market on V3 and V4](https://x.com/aave/status/2115937863162339637) as a precaution — withdrawals and repayments stayed open — and unfroze them the next day; Oct 30 brought the period's biggest one-day exits, led by Aave on Ethereum (USDC −$331.8M, USDT −$188.3M), partly reversed after. Huma, Sky, Ethena, Re, Cap, 3Jane, Jupiter and Kamino all said they had no exposure.
- **Biggest mover down:** Sky sUSDS **−$539.5M (−10.75%)**, three weeks down; its single biggest day, −$219.3M on Oct 28, came the day before the incident. No public explanation found.
- **The market shrank, faster each week:** stablecoin supply **−$8.26B (−2.68%)** — −$1.43B, then −$2.32B, then −$4.51B in the incident week. Tracked lending venues lost **−$872.4M (−7.02%)**. Arc was the only chain growing at scale (**+$588.4M, +115.58%**).
- **Huma:** PST **−$30.9M (−7.71%)** to $369.9M, nearly all in the week of Oct 19–26 (−$30.2M, including −$20.3M of reserves on Oct 21). PST collateral in the tracked Kamino market halved (−$19.1M) as RockawayX wound down its Kamino loops — while Sentora [opened a new Sentora Huma PST market on Kamino](https://x.com/humafinance/status/2115793051679613211) that [reached $10M within days](https://x.com/kamino/status/2116954271199449502). Huma [crossed $20B in payment volume](https://x.com/humafinance/status/2113309154337247579).
- **Launch of the period:** tokenized stocks became borrowable collateral across DeFi — [Robinhood Stock Tokens on Morpho](https://x.com/Morpho/status/2113269057965404398), [Backpack's tokenized equities on Kamino](https://x.com/kamino/status/2115467786189459909) and [24/7 stock pricing coming to Aave](https://x.com/aave/status/2115814972697780226) — and [Aave V4 passed $1.5B in deposits](https://x.com/aave/status/2114745973201060014).

## What drove the big moves?

**The Binance incident (Oct 29–30) — what the protocols did.** Aave [froze all DAO-operated markets on V3 and V4](https://x.com/aave/status/2115937863162339637), pausing new deposits and borrows while withdrawals and repayments continued, then [unfroze everything on Oct 30](https://x.com/aave/status/2116167595237720167), saying its contracts were unaffected and positions stayed collateralised. A freeze that leaves only the exit open turns caution into one-way flow, and the Oct 30 snapshot shows it across Aave: USDC −$331.8M and USDT −$188.3M on Ethereum, USDT0 −$76.5M on Plasma and −$41.8M on Mantle, and USDC −$54.3M on Aave V4 on Arc — most of it partly reversed after the unfreeze. Other venues moved the same day: the Steakhouse USDG vault on Robinhood −$28.6M, Paypal USD Main on Morpho −$29.4M and syrupUSDC on Aave on Monad −$17.2M. Jupiter Lend reported [a liquidity crunch that briefly blocked USDC withdrawals](https://x.com/jupiter_earn/status/2116093239254208923), [cleared the same day](https://x.com/jupiter_earn/status/2116293104768778324) as borrowers repaid. Kamino [paused borrowing in its SOL/BTC market](https://x.com/kamino/status/2115927540212650346) and [resumed it](https://x.com/kamino/status/2116181028939223478); Re [paused reUSD redemptions](https://x.com/re/status/2115923157572079657) and [resumed them on Oct 30](https://x.com/re/status/2116218781609717831). Ethena put its exposure at [about $8.8M of unrealised PnL against a reserve fund above $60M](https://x.com/ethena/status/2115914824198595026) and published a [proof of reserves showing USDe over 101% collateralised](https://x.com/ethena/status/2116023705939878311). Separately, a [Railway hosting breach on Oct 22](https://x.com/USDai_Official/status/2113115109006409935) had several teams rotating credentials; none reported losses.

**Sky — sUSDS −$539.5M (−10.75%).** Three weeks down, −$151.5M in week 1 and −$344.5M in week 3, with the single biggest day −$219.3M on Oct 28 — before the Binance news — and the savings rate unchanged at 3.80%. USDS on Ethereum fell −4.52% in DefiLlama's live week. Sky's posts cover [Q3 Net Revenue up 11.8%](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2115792725500948921) and say [$8.9B of its $9.63B in allocations is redeemable within a week](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2114722524043030548) (Sky's figures), and that it had [no exposure to Binance](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2115925551403978760). No public explanation found for the outflow; over the month sUSDS is −$179.1M (−3.84%).

**Ethena — USDe −$220.2M (−4.63%), and USDe leaving Solana.** A third week down, −$100.5M of it in week 2, the biggest day −$51.9M on Oct 28, while APY edged up from 4.87% to 5.09%. The clearest flow is out of Solana lending: Kamino's USDe market lost −$115.3M (−71.03%) and Jupiter Lend's −$55.3M, with Aave on Ethereum −$34.8M and Plasma sUSDe −$24.2M. Ethena meanwhile launched [Earn+, up to 12% on EthenaPay](https://x.com/ethena/status/2115413879840989651) and [USDe on OKX's X Layer with an Aave instance](https://x.com/ethena/status/2113573498694336843). *Possible cause:* leveraged USDe positions continuing to unwind after September's end of USDe incentives, then the incident week (evidence: three weeks of steady outflows from the loop markets).

**USDai — sUSDai −$106.9M (−19.56%), almost all on one day.** −$118.0M on Nov 1, which is sUSDai's [fixed monthly redemption date](https://x.com/USDai_Official/status/2108586988551213309) — USDai's own post says [holders who stayed through the Nov 1 redemption earned about 8.04%](https://x.com/USDai_Official/status/2117091189300375698). It came after the [CHIP token airdrop claims opened on Oct 20](https://x.com/USDai_Official/status/2112551723772637611) and a [proposal setting a 7% minimum sUSDai APY](https://x.com/USDai_Official/status/2113645760634097937). *Possible cause:* holders redeeming once the airdrop was claimed (evidence: timing). Fluid's sUSDai market on Arbitrum fell −$10.2M.

**Cap — stcUSD −$83.2M (−30.64%).** Two-thirds of it in one day, −$54.8M on Oct 12; APY fell from 6.35% to 5.39%. As before, the row is DefiLlama's protocol-wide TVL, mostly collateral delegated to Cap's borrowers rather than stcUSD itself. Cap later [removed deposit and withdrawal fees on its USDC route](https://x.com/CapApp/status/2114724080683511896). No public explanation found for Oct 12.

**Re — reUSD +$53.1M (+17.90%), reUSDe +$9.2M (+43.75%).** The period's biggest grower, up three weeks with APY steady around 6.88%. Re [crossed $350M in deposits](https://x.com/re/status/2112199092923437182), [deployed $65M of new capital into Cover Re](https://x.com/re/status/2112261615991267462) and opened [PT-reUSD looping on Monad](https://x.com/re/status/2115445876810543151). *Possible cause:* new looping venues and steady reinsurance yield (evidence: timing and the flat APY).

**Morpho — Ethereum −$126.9M (−9.52%), Robinhood −$31.1M, Tempo −$17.0M.** On Ethereum, Paypal USD Main fell −$70.4M and Sentora RLUSD Main −$48.1M (its APY from 6.90% to 5.91%). In the same period Sentora began [curating Aave V4's first external markets, lending RLUSD, PYUSD and OUSD](https://x.com/aave/status/2113369853906276557) — *possible cause:* stablecoin supply following the curator to the new venue (evidence: timing only). Sentora pathUSD on Tempo lost −$17.0M with its APY at 0.00%, which reads as a vault being wound down. No public explanation found for the Robinhood outflow beyond the Oct 30 day (−$28.6M).

**Solana lending — Kamino −$157.4M (−51.96%), Jupiter Lend −$59.0M (−14.94%).** USDe drove both (above); Kamino also lost PST collateral (−$19.1M) and syrupUSDC (−$12.9M). Kamino's growth went elsewhere, outside the markets Huma Radar tracks: its [AUTO market passed $170M](https://x.com/kamino/status/2113016641323733341) and [fixed-rate loans $75M](https://x.com/kamino/status/2116539723611025722).

**Smaller moves.** Spark spUSDS −$25.6M is a lending pool (◇) — $1.22B supplied against $886.7M borrowed (live) — and Spark has no X account configured here. 3Jane USD3 +$13.7M (+14.79%), almost all on Oct 26, as it launched [promise looping](https://x.com/3janexyz/status/2110787930163986611). Yuzu syzUSD −$15.3M, −$12.4M of it on Nov 2 on Plasma, and Avant savUSD −$9.4M, three weeks down; no public explanation found for either.
