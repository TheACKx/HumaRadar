**Window:** snapshot 2026-09-18 → 2026-09-28 (10 days) · MoM from 2026-08-29 · **Source:** [Huma Radar](https://humaradar.theack.workers.dev) snapshots, live DefiLlama for chain and per-stablecoin splits, the protocols' X accounts · **Numbers:** `reports/data/2026-09-28.tables.md`

> The first weekly report. It covers ten days, 18–28 September, so its change columns read **10D** instead of WoW; from here on reports run Monday to Monday. The 28 September figures are the day's intraday snapshot.

## TL;DR

- **Biggest mover up:** Ethena USDe **+$165.7M (+3.50%)**, its third straight week up and +$825.5M over the month, as USDe became the collateral behind [two of Morpho's three largest markets](https://x.com/ethena/status/2102011157129343125).
- **Biggest mover down:** Aave v3 on Ethereum **−$332.5M**, almost all one USDT exit — **−$243.7M on Sep 19** with borrowing flat — which left the reserve at 93.0% utilisation. No public explanation found.
- **Fastest-growing stablecoin chain:** Solana **+$1.01B (+6.42%)** by dollars, Tempo **+78.31%** by rate. Arc went the other way in its second week: **−$189.0M (−29.21%)**, almost all USDC.
- **Huma:** PST **+$49.1M (+14.76%)** to $381.8M, outgrowing stablecoin supply on both of its chains. Tracked venues added only **+$1.0M**, and part of Ethereum's gain was PST moving over from Solana — **≈ $25.9M on Sep 22**.
- **Launch of the week:** tokenized stocks got a U.S. trading path with the SEC's [Innovation Exemption](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment) (Sep 17), and ARK's venture fund [went onchain through Securitize](https://x.com/Securitize/status/2103107371362873543) (Sep 24).

## What drove the big moves?

**Ethena — USDe +$165.7M (+3.50%).** A third straight week of growth, +$825.5M over the month, with APY barely moving (4.68% → 4.76%) — so yield isn't what's pulling it. Most of the rise landed in the Sep 19–22 snapshots, led by a +$69.8M day. The clearest driver on record is USDe as collateral: Ethena said on Sep 21 that [two of Morpho's three largest markets are now backed by USDe](https://x.com/ethena/status/2102011157129343125), and borrowing against USDe in the Morpho markets listed under the venue table kept climbing — $331.5M → $350.8M on Base, $298.3M → $304.5M on Robinhood. On Sep 25 Ethena began [extending its basis trade to equity perpetuals on Binance](https://x.com/ethena/status/2103439484113346986), with allocations starting that day. *Possible cause:* borrowing demand against USDe on Morpho (evidence: Ethena's post and the borrow figures above); the Binance change fits the later growth on timing only. Binance Wallet's [Hold to Earn](https://x.com/ethena/status/2102724151920468164) (Sep 23) didn't coincide with growth. Headwind ahead: Ethena says [token incentives tied to USDe end with this month](https://x.com/ethena/status/2103758327142641925).

**Aave — two flagged groups, and neither is Aave V4.**

- *v3 on Ethereum −$332.5M* is mostly one USDT event: −$281.5M (85% of the move), −$243.7M of it on Sep 19, with borrowing flat ($2.71B → $2.69B). One large supplier leaving is the likeliest reading; no public explanation found. It didn't go to Aave V4: LlamaRisk's reports show V4's whole Ethereum Core Hub grew only from about $446M on Sep 16 to $487M on Sep 25 ([Sep 16](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-aave-v4-activation-on-ethereum-mainnet/24293/51), [Sep 25](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-aave-v4-activation-on-ethereum-mainnet/24293/52)). The exit pushed utilisation from 85.2% to 93.0% — exactly the new kink, since risk stewards [lowered USDT's optimal utilisation from 94% to 93% on Sep 16](https://governance.aave.com/t/risk-stewards-cap-and-irm-changes-on-aave-v3-2026-09-16/25653) — so the supply APY rise (3.14% → 3.80%) is mechanical, and it is the kind of rate that tends to pull deposits back.
- *The USDe side is loops unwinding.* sUSDe fell −$41.6M (a third week down) and USDe −$20.0M, with USDe borrowed on the reserve dropping $90.1M → $43.5M, after Aave had priced USDe debt above what sUSDe yields and [cut the Core USDe borrow cap from 700M to 100M on Sep 22](https://governance.aave.com/t/risk-stewards-supply-and-borrow-cap-changes-on-aave-v3-2026-09-22/25685). The −$19.2M USDe day, Sep 26, is the day Ethena [said its USDe incentives end](https://x.com/ethena/status/2103758327142641925). *Possible cause:* leveraged sUSDe loops closing as they stopped paying (evidence: the rate and cap changes, the falling USDe debt, the timing).
- *v3 on Monad −$43.1M* — USDT0 −$21.9M and USDe −$20.4M, both concentrated on Sep 21, the pattern of a loop being closed. It is part of stablecoins draining from Monad (the chain's supply −$26.6M): Aave's risk stewards noted in September that [incentives, not the organic rate, keep stablecoin supply there, and large suppliers had already left](https://governance.aave.com/t/risk-stewards-monad-stablecoin-irm-adjustments-slope1-to-5-00/25622). No public explanation found for Sep 21. The USDC supply APY's fall (9.47% → 5.32%) overstates the change: the start was a one-day spike at 94.3% utilisation.

**Sky — sUSDS +$80.9M is Galaxy.** Nearly all of it is one reading: +$104.0M in the Sep 24 snapshot, the first after Galaxy [added $100M of sUSDS to its treasury and approved it as collateral for its institutional clients](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sky-protocol-and-galaxy-digital-announce-strategic-partnership-across-lending-and-capital-markets-302887725.html) on Sep 23 ([Sky's post](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2102751922516337085)). *Possible cause:* Galaxy's purchase (evidence: the size and the timing). The rest of the period was a small net outflow, with APY flat at 3.60%. The month's −$237.0M comes almost entirely from one reading before this window, on Sep 16 ([pool history](https://yields.llama.fi/chart/d8c4eff5-c8a9-46fc-a888-057c4c668e72)) — no public explanation found.

**Spark — spUSDS −$26.9M is borrowing, not withdrawals.** The row is a lending pool (◇), so it shows available liquidity. SparkLend's USDS borrowing rose from $889.6M to $980.5M over the window while supply rose from $1,355.8M to $1,421.8M — borrowing outgrew deposits ([DefiLlama](https://api.llama.fi/protocol/sparklend)). Sky says Galaxy [already borrows on Spark](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2102751930367836431) for its onchain financing product. *Possible cause:* rising USDS borrowing demand, Galaxy's included (evidence: the borrow figures and Sky's post). APY rose 2.32% → 2.56%. No public explanation found for the −$41.3M dip on Sep 23.

**Morpho — Ethereum +$68.1M, Robinhood +$38.3M, Base +$30.0M, Monad −$6.0M.**

- *Ethereum* is two Sentora vaults and one day: RLUSD Main +$37.3M (+$51.5M on Sep 26) and Prime Main +$25.2M (+$28.1M the same day). Onchain it was a handful of large deposits on Sep 25 — two RLUSD deposits of $37.4M and $37.6M five minutes apart, against a $33.1M withdrawal ([RLUSD Main](https://app.morpho.org/ethereum/vault/0x6dC58a0FdfC8D694e571DC59B9A52EEEa780E6bf/sentora-rlusd-main)), and PYUSD deposits of $18.0M and $10.0M that evening ([Prime Main](https://app.morpho.org/ethereum/vault/0xC21b08C16458202593D4D9B26b9984Ee67b38BbD/sentora-prime-main)) — large allocators, not broad demand. No Sentora, Ripple or PayPal announcement found.
- *Robinhood* +$38.3M is the Steakhouse USDG vault behind Robinhood Earn, which Morpho says [has drawn $500M+ in deposits in three months](https://x.com/Morpho/status/2103577514853732656) (Sep 25). A third week up, spread across the window (biggest day +$11.5M) — steady retail inflow.
- *Base* +$30.0M is Steakhouse High Yield USDC Edition, one of the two Morpho vaults behind [Coinbase's USDC lending](https://kitchen.steakhouse.financial/p/lend-your-usdc-on-coinbase), also a third week up and spread out (biggest day +$9.3M). Coinbase [extended the product to Brazil](https://crypto.news/coinbase-expands-morpho-powered-usdc-lending-to-brazil/) on Sep 9.
- These are the vaults' own deposits. The markets they lend into — USDe / USDC on Base (+$21.1M), USDe / USDG and syrupUSDG / USDG on Robinhood — hold the same money, so this report lists them apart instead of adding them on top (see the Data notes).
- *Monad* −$6.0M: August USDC V2 −$4.6M (−$3.7M of it on Sep 24) and Hyperithm −$1.4M, part of the same drain from Monad as Aave's. No public explanation found.

**Cap — stcUSD −$36.2M, which isn't stcUSD.** The row is DefiLlama's protocol-wide TVL, and most of it is collateral that restakers delegate to Cap's borrowers, not stcUSD. The drop is that collateral leaving: delegated uniBTC fell from 2,516.8 to 1,675.7 tokens on Sep 23–25 (about $53M), and on Sep 25 — the row's −$46.9M day — borrowed USDC fell from $66.9M to $60.5M ([DefiLlama token breakdown](https://api.llama.fi/protocol/cap)). stcUSD itself grew over the window, from about $80.4M to $85.2M in market cap ([CoinGecko](https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/staked-cap-usd/market_chart?vs_currency=usd&days=14&interval=daily)). *Possible cause:* a borrower repaid and its underwriter's collateral was released — in Cap's design [each delegation backs one borrower](https://docs.cap.app/concepts/delegation/symbiotic) (evidence: the same-day repayment). The APY fall (6.35% → 5.51%) fits less borrowing. No public explanation found for the exit itself.

**Jupiter Lend — USDe −$54.1M on Solana.** Nearly all of it is the USDe market (−$54.4M), where borrowing is negligible, so this is supply leaving. That USDe backs a curated [USDe loop that borrows USDG against it](https://jup.ag/lend/ethena/market), so an outflow is loops unwinding. The biggest day, −$18.7M on Sep 26, is the day Ethena [said its USDe incentives end](https://x.com/ethena/status/2103758327142641925) — and the day Aave's USDe reserve on Ethereum lost −$19.2M. *Possible cause:* loopers exiting as incentives end (evidence: timing). Not every USDe market shrank — USDe / USDC on Morpho Base still added +$21.1M over the period — and the outflows before Sep 26 aren't explained.

**Fluid — USDC −$17.8M on Ethereum, sUSDai −$11.8M on Arbitrum.** The Ethereum USDC market is total supplied, a real deposit figure, and it fell in steps — the largest −$10.6M on Sep 22 — while its APY rose 3.84% → 4.42% as supply thinned: the exits pushed the rate up, not the reverse. No public explanation found; Fluid's [blog](https://fluid.io/blog) has nothing since August. The Arbitrum sUSDai outflow lines up with sUSDai's Solana launch: USD.AI [brought USDai and sUSDai to Solana on Sep 24](https://usd.ai/insights/usdai-expands-on-solana) with incentives, and Jupiter Lend opened [sUSDai vaults with loops quoted up to 35% APY](https://x.com/jupiter_earn/status/2103198313130664036), which it says [passed $15M in three days](https://x.com/jupiter_earn/status/2104467964213002731). *Possible cause:* loopers moving to the new Solana markets (evidence: timing, and Jupiter's figure). sUSDai itself grew +$16.2M (+3.27%), so the money moved rather than left.

**Re — reUSD +$16.0M (+6.14%).** A third straight week up with APY flat at 6.75%, so not yield. Re kept adding places to use reUSD: [Exponent markets with 30x Re Points](https://x.com/re/status/2100587178116120861) (Sep 17), [reUSD vaults on Fluid Arbitrum with 10x–20x points](https://x.com/re/status/2103148309246570615) (Sep 24), and a USDC / PT-reUSD market on Morpho that [passed $100M in supply](https://x.com/re/status/2102487174751146200) (Sep 22). *Possible cause:* points-boosted integrations and leveraged PT-reUSD positions drawing new mints (evidence: launch timing). Re says total deposits [passed $312M on Sep 25](https://x.com/re/status/2103546944668975439), counted differently from the reUSD pool above.

**Tori — strUSD +$7.1M (+10.40%).** A third week up, with APY rising 10.63% → 12.02%. The growth came as fresh mints from Sep 23, just as DefiLlama [added trUSD's Pharos deployment, bridged over Chainlink CCIP](https://github.com/DefiLlama/peggedassets-server/pull/929), and a [Morpho strUSD market on Monad](https://yields.llama.fi/chart/fa7bb2b9-86fd-542f-b00a-6dc6dc7c763c) grew from $2.2M to $6.3M. *Possible cause:* new places to use strUSD plus the higher yield (evidence: timing only). No announcement found; Tori's X account, [@tori_finance](https://x.com/tori_finance), isn't configured yet.

## Data notes

- **Ten-day window.** 18 → 28 Sep: change columns read 10D, and MoM compares with Aug 29. Reports run Monday to Monday from here on. The supply table's top-3 and biggest-mover columns are DefiLlama's live seven-day figures, not ten.
- **Sep 28 is intraday.** The end point is the 12:00 UTC snapshot, and DefiLlama revises the current day until midnight UTC, so end-day figures can still move — Ethena's was falling as it was read. A move dated Sep 26 happened in the 24 hours before that snapshot (the Sentora deposits were made on Sep 25).
- **Vault-funded Morpho markets are no longer counted twice.** A curated vault lends its deposits into Morpho markets, and the venue totals used to add both the vault and those markets. Eight markets funded at least 90% by tracked vaults — $1.08B — are now listed apart under the venue table, which is why tracked lending TVL reads $12.21B.
- **Left out of the tables** (config `exclude`): Ondo USDY and Hastra PRIME, which have had no TVL from DefiLlama since Sep 16, and Orca, an AMM pool rather than a lending venue (it stays in Huma's PST liquidity). All three are still on the site.
- **Cap's row isn't stcUSD.** It is DefiLlama's protocol TVL, mostly restaked collateral delegated to Cap's borrowers (uniBTC, LBTC, wstETH); stcUSD itself was about $85.2M on Sep 28 ([CoinGecko](https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/staked-cap-usd/market_chart?vs_currency=usd&days=14&interval=daily)). Tracking stcUSD's market cap, as Maple now is, would make the row mean what it says.
- **Maple is token supply** (syrupUSDC + syrupUSDT + syrupUSDG market caps), not DefiLlama's Maple yields pool, which also counts loan collateral.
- **◇ rows** (Spark spUSDS, Aave USDC) are available liquidity, not deposits.
- **Aave v4 on Arc** is tracked from Sep 24, so it has no 10D change; the venue total's change is like for like (‡) without it.
- **Snapshot vs. live drift (WARN).** BSC's live supply is 3.12% above the snapshot because of the USDX move above; Monad drifts 1.64%.
