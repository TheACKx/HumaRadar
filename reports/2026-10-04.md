## What drove the big moves?

**Sky — sUSDS +$190.4M (+4.25%).** Two-thirds of it arrived in one reading, +$125.1M on Sep 29, with APY flat at 3.60% — so not a rate change. Sky posted on Sep 30 that [USDS supply was back above $10B after a $237M rise in 24 hours](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2105237286749364493), which fits the timing; USDS was also among Ethereum's top three stablecoins, +4.16% in DefiLlama's live week. Sky didn't say who minted or deposited. *Possible cause:* one or a few large depositors (evidence: the single-day jump and Sky's supply post). Over the month sUSDS is only +$12.0M (+0.26%): this week won back mid-September's drop. Separately, S&P [published its annual review of Sky with a stable outlook](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2105969433504084150) (Oct 2).

**Ethena — USDe −$43.7M (−0.88%), and USDe moving around Solana.** The product fell in one reading, −$44.4M on Sep 29, a small dip after a month of +$640.2M (+15.01%). Ethena had said [token incentives tied to USDe would end with September](https://x.com/ethena/status/2105623771260543190), and APY eased from 5.10% to 4.96%. *Possible cause:* incentive-driven deposits leaving as rewards ended (evidence: timing only). On Solana, USDe swapped venues rather than leaving: Kamino's USDe reserve lost −$34.7M (biggest day −$21.8M on Oct 1) while Jupiter Lend's USDe market gained +$41.8M (biggest day +$17.7M on Oct 2) — last week Jupiter had lost −$54.1M of it. Neither Kamino's nor Jupiter's posts mention USDe; no public explanation found for the switch.

**Morpho — Ethereum +$37.4M, Robinhood +$27.7M, Monad +$13.1M.**

- *Ethereum* nets a PYUSD switch and an RLUSD inflow. Paypal USD Main fell −$46.9M (biggest day −$18.9M on Oct 2) while Sentora Huma PST Main, which also takes PYUSD, rose +$23.7M (+$19.6M on Oct 2). Huma said that day that Sentora had [raised the vault's capacity, drawing $68M of PYUSD](https://x.com/humafinance/status/2106068259124707475). *Possible cause:* PYUSD moving to the vault paying more — 7.89% against 5.57% (evidence: same asset, same day). Sentora RLUSD Main added +$51.6M, +$36.9M of it on Oct 2, while its APY fell from 6.09% to 5.31%; no public explanation found.
- *Robinhood* +$27.7M is again the Steakhouse USDG vault behind Robinhood Earn, a third week up and spread across the days (biggest day +$8.6M). Morpho says its credit network on Robinhood Chain went [from zero to $1B in deposits since July](https://x.com/Morpho/status/2104964818696552853) — steady retail inflow.
- *Monad* +$13.1M is mostly Hyperithm USDC Apex (+$11.6M, biggest day +$7.4M on Sep 29), with its APY falling from 9.58% to 7.31% as deposits came in. No public explanation found.

**Aave — Monad +$41.7M, and Ethereum USDC liquidity −$16.3M.**

- *v3 on Monad* reversed last week's outflow. USDC added +$33.4M (+$26.0M on Sep 30) and borrowing rose with it ($133.2M → $160.2M), while the supply APY fell from 7.20% to 4.13%; USDe added +$16.9M, almost all on Oct 1. USDT0 went the other way, −$14.5M, leaving its utilisation at 94.2% and its APY from 4.05% to 9.21%. Monad's stablecoin supply rose +$23.7M (+3.36%) on the week, led by USDC. *Possible cause:* a high USDC rate drawing new money onto Monad (evidence: the rate fell as supply arrived). No announcement found; Aave's only Monad news was a [proposal to deploy Aave V4 there](https://x.com/aave/status/2104973840552219046).
- *Aave USDC −$16.3M (−9.83%)* is a lending pool (◇): the figure is available liquidity, and the reserve has $2.41B supplied against $2.26B borrowed (live). The whole move came in one day (−$16.6M on Oct 1) — either borrowers drew more or a supplier left; the tables don't split it. Aave v3 on Ethereum as a whole moved only −$4.1M (−0.07%). No public explanation found.

**Jupiter Lend — +$36.3M on Solana.** The USDe market above (+$41.8M) explains all of it. The one shrinking market was PST / JupUSD, −$6.1M, with its borrowing falling from $32.1M to $27.0M — PST loops partly unwound (see Huma below).

**Kamino — −$47.5M on Solana.** USDe −$34.7M (covered above) and syrupUSDC −$13.5M (biggest day −$4.0M on Oct 1). syrupUSDC also fell across Maple's own supply (−$5.6M, −0.56%). Kamino's posts this week are all about its new fixed-rate loans; no public explanation found.

**3Jane — USD3 +$14.0M (+17.76%).** Almost all on Sep 29 (+$13.7M). The night before, 3Jane [raised USD3's supply cap to $100M for the next major U.S. fintech originator going live](https://x.com/3janexyz/status/2104683702458699951), and USD3 now sits at $92.6M — close to that cap. 3Jane later said [USD3 funds about 25,000 consumer and small-business loans](https://x.com/3janexyz/status/2106115341718098134). Cause: the cap raise.

**Cap — stcUSD −$18.1M (−6.21%).** Mostly one day, −$14.1M on Oct 2, while APY rose from 4.84% to 7.54%. As last week, the row is DefiLlama's protocol-wide TVL, mostly collateral delegated to Cap's borrowers rather than stcUSD itself, so the drop need not be stcUSD holders leaving. Cap posted nothing in the window; no public explanation found.
