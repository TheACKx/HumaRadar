## What drove the big moves?

**Ethena and Aave — the USDe unwind.** USDe left lending venues across the board: Aave v3 on Ethereum lost −$238.1M of USDe (biggest day −$84.5M on Oct 7) and Kamino on Solana −$48.2M, while Ethena's protocol-wide USDe fell −$146.3M, −$88.8M of it on Oct 8. On Aave the USDe reserve is almost all collateral — USDe borrowed there barely moved ($16.0M → $15.6M) — and in the same week USDT borrowing on Aave fell from $2.69B to $2.48B. Collateral leaving while the stablecoin borrowed against it is repaid is what closing a USDe/USDT loop looks like. *Possible cause:* loopers exiting in the first week without USDe incentives, which Ethena [ended from the start of October](https://x.com/ethena/status/2105623771260543190), while USDe's own yield slipped from 5.36% to 4.86% (evidence: the matching collateral and borrow falls, the timing). Not all USDe left lending: Jupiter Lend on Solana added +$70.0M of it and Aave v3 on Monad +$31.9M. Early investors' last ENA tokens also [unlocked on Oct 5](https://x.com/TheDeFinvestor/status/2106721190383439980). On Aave's USDT side, utilisation fell from 93.6% to 90.6% and the supply APY from 3.76% to 3.51% as borrowers repaid.

**Sky — sUSDS +$272.5M (+5.84%).** A third week up and +$337.0M over the month. Sky [raised the Sky Savings Rate from 3.60% to 3.80%](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2107880367579418824) on Oct 7, and USDS on Ethereum grew +4.83% in DefiLlama's live week. The biggest day, +$115.2M on Oct 5, came two days before that announcement, so the rate explains the later inflows at most. In the same week Sky became [the first stablecoin protocol rated by both Moody's and S&P](https://x.com/SkyEcosystem/status/2108134721930592711). *Possible cause:* institutional demand for a rated, higher-yielding savings token (evidence: the rate change and the ratings on timing; no single depositor named).

**Maple — syrupUSD +$86.3M (+5.87%).** All three tokens grew: syrupUSDC +$50.0M, syrupUSDT +$18.1M and syrupUSDG +$18.1M, with the biggest day +$34.8M on Oct 10. Maple opened new places to use them that week — [syrupUSDG went live on Arbitrum](https://x.com/maplefinance/status/2107471213438329128) and syrupUSDC became [collateral on Aave V4 on Arc](https://x.com/aave/status/2107864585571356735) — and reported [its fifth straight month of revenue growth](https://x.com/maplefinance/status/2107445487519560014). *Possible cause:* the new venues (evidence: timing).

**Spark — spUSDS −$63.0M (−14.46%) is liquidity, not deposits.** The row is a lending pool (◇): available liquidity, with $1.19B supplied against $822.4M borrowed (live). It fell in steps, the biggest −$26.3M on Oct 6, while APY rose from 2.56% to 2.70%, which suggests borrowing grew faster than supply. Spark has no X account or blog configured here; no public explanation found.

**Jupiter Lend and Kamino — USDe changes venue on Solana again.** Jupiter Lend on Solana +$68.5M (+20.98%) is all its USDe market (+$70.0M, biggest day +$31.0M on Oct 6), while Kamino on Solana −$56.6M (−15.48%) is mostly USDe (−$48.2M), plus PST collateral −$4.0M and USDC −$3.8M. It is the third week of USDe swinging between the two. Fluid says Jupiter Lend [crossed $1B in active loans](https://x.com/0xfluid/status/2108818198720369094), the largest lending market on Solana. Neither protocol's posts mention USDe; no public explanation found for the switch.

**Aave v3 on Monad — +$46.1M (+9.67%).** USDe +$31.9M (+$23.0M on Oct 8) and USDT0 +$12.6M, the USDT0 inflow taking its utilisation from 94.2% to 75.7% and its APY from 9.21% to 2.84%; GHO −$5.0M. Monad's stablecoin supply rose +$25.4M, with USDe on Monad +46.03% in DefiLlama's live week, around the [Monad Summit on Oct 6](https://x.com/TheDeFinvestor/status/2106721190383439980). No public explanation found.

**Morpho — Ethereum +$35.5M (+2.75%).** Paypal USD Main came back +$36.5M (biggest day +$14.4M on Oct 5) after last week's outflow, its APY up from 5.57% to 5.94%; Sentora Huma PST Main added +$4.6M and Sentora RLUSD Main −$6.8M. No public explanation found.

**Fluid — USDC +$9.3M (+7.07%), and reUSD out on Ethereum.** The USDC market's APY jumped from 4.45% to 6.43%, and Fluid has been [advertising about 6% on USDC](https://x.com/0xfluid/status/2108875191212445899) — the higher rate is the likely draw (*possible cause*, evidence: the rate move). Fluid on Ethereum −$6.3M (−7.53%) is reUSD (−$6.4M), in the week Jupiter Lend [opened a reUSD loop on Solana](https://x.com/jupiter_earn/status/2107838339407880233) — a link on timing only.

**Aave USDC −$8.6M (−5.73%)** is a lending pool (◇): the reserve has $2.42B supplied against $2.27B borrowed (live), and the week's biggest day, −$21.0M on Oct 5, was partly reversed. No public explanation found.
